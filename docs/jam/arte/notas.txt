== Prompt base (bloque de estilo agregado a cover, keyart g1 y banner) ==
Style: stylized painted video-game key art. The character must match the reference image exactly: a young director with a large stylized head and compact body, clean low-poly faceted 3D look with flat colors and simple facets — dark grey newsboy cap, brown hair, knee-length open grey trench coat with flap pockets, olive green buttoned vest over a light shirt, blue-grey scarf wrapped around the neck with two tails, dark trousers, brown laced boots. Background painted like a stylized illustrated matte painting (not photorealistic): deep navy-blue rainy night, art-deco skyline silhouettes with a spire like the Empire State building, rooftop water towers, fire escapes, warm amber lit windows, a glowing full moon behind wispy clouds, diagonal streaks of rain. Color language: warm yellow = light, violet = darkness/shadows, cyan = rewind. Absolutely NO text, NO letters, NO words, NO numbers anywhere in the image (the marquee sign and the clapperboard must be blank, no writing), no logos, no watermark.
