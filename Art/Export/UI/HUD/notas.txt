-----
A 2x2 sprite sheet of four video game HUD ability icons for "The Director's Cut", a 1940s film-noir 2.5D game. Match the style of the attached reference icon: flat stylized low-poly facets, simple flat colors, minimal texture, clean vector look.

Strict layout: the square image is split into four equal quadrants. Each quadrant contains exactly ONE icon, centered, filling about 75% of its quadrant, with generous empty margin so icons never touch or overlap each other or the image border. NO grid lines, NO frames, NO tiles, NO text, NO letters, NO labels, NO numbers.

Background: one single perfectly flat solid pure green color (#00FF00) across the whole image, no gradient, no shadow, no vignette, no texture.

Shared style for all four icons (must be identical): bold thick near-black outline (#111118) of the same width around every icon's silhouette, like a sticker; the same lighting from the top-left with one flat lighter facet and one flat darker facet; bold simple chunky shapes readable at 48 pixels; no glow or haze extending outside the black outline (any light beam is a solid flat shape enclosed by the black outline).

Top-left: a vintage film studio spotlight (fresnel stage light on a short yoke), dark gunmetal gray body, turned ON, shining a solid bright warm yellow (#FFC83A) cone-shaped beam pointing to the lower right, the beam drawn as a flat yellow wedge with a black outline.

Top-right: the EXACT same spotlight with the same angle and shape, but turned OFF: lens dark, no beam; instead a solid violet/purple (#8A4FFF) crescent moon shape and small violet shadow wedge where the beam would be, outlined in black.

Bottom-left: a film reel (movie reel with round holes) in dark gray, wrapped by a bold thick circular counter-clockwise rewind arrow in bright neon cyan (#2EE6F0) with a big arrowhead, outlined in black.

Bottom-right: a movie clapperboard snapping shut, white and gray body with black-and-white diagonal striped clapper stick at an angle mid-close, with a small sharp white four-point star spark flash at the hinge tip, mostly white and gray, outlined in black.
